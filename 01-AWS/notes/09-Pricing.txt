AWS Pricing Options

On-Demand
- Pay for what you use.
- No long-term commitment.

Reserved
- Commit to a longer term for eligible resources.
- Lower cost than On-Demand.

Savings Plans
- Commit to a consistent amount of usage.
- Can provide lower cost than On-Demand.

Spot
- Uses spare AWS capacity.
- Lower cost, but workloads can be interrupted.
