Regions, Availability Zones and Edge

Region
- Geographic location.
- Contains multiple Availability Zones.

Availability Zone (AZ)
- Separate data center location(s) within a Region.
- Helps with high availability and fault isolation.

Edge Location
- Location closer to users.
- Helps reduce latency by serving cached/delivered content.

Choose a Region based on:
- Latency
- Pricing
- Service availability
- Data compliance
- Proximity
